## 🛡️ OpenProxy Shield Privacy Policy (Full Text)

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Developer:** PocketESP-IDE / OpenProxy Shield Team  
**Contact Email:** [maker@pocketesp.com](mailto:maker@pocketesp.com)

### 1. Zero-Log Guarantee
OpenProxy Shield does NOT inspect, monitor, track, store, or log:
- Websites visited or browsed
- Traffic payloads, downloaded files, or session content
- Real origin IP addresses
- Personal user accounts (no login or email registration required)

### 2. VPN Tunnel & Network Routing
OpenProxy Shield utilizes Android's native `VpnService` to route user-authorized network traffic through remote proxy nodes (SOCKS5/HTTPS).
- Encrypted DNS requests are directed to public resolvers (8.8.8.8) to prevent ISP DNS poisoning.
- Local configuration settings (such as custom ports and session timers) are stored strictly on the local device via `SharedPreferences`.

### 3. Advertising & Google AdMob
OpenProxy Shield is supported by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) and crash/diagnostic data under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Users can reset or delete their advertising identifier at any time via Android Settings.

### 4. Children's Privacy & User Rights
This application does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Users have full autonomy under GDPR and CCPA.
