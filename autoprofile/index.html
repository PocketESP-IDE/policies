Privacy Policy for AutoProfile

Effective Date: October 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to AutoProfile ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Amit. AutoProfile is designed from the ground up with a strict privacy-first, on-device automation architecture.

Our Core Privacy Principles:

  • No Cloud Tracking: We operate no external databases tracking your identity, profile triggers, or daily habits.
  • No Account Required: No login, phone number, email registration, or social sign-in is required to use AutoProfile.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal data, location, or schedule to advertising brokers or analytics firms.

This Privacy Policy explains what permissions AutoProfile requests, why each permission is technically necessary for automated sound profiling, and how user privacy is safeguarded in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (including the Sensitive Data & Background Location policies).

2. Sensitive Permissions & Data Processing

All rule evaluations and profile triggers occur 100% locally on your device using a private, sandboxed Android Room database.

A. Location Data (Foreground & Background)

  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION.
  • Purpose: To allow you to designate geographic geofence zones (such as "Work", "College", "Home", or "Place of Worship") that automatically toggle your phone's ringer (Silent, Vibrate, Normal) upon arrival or departure.
  • Background Requirement: Background location is necessary so your sound profiles trigger reliably even when your phone is locked in your pocket or when other apps are active.
  • Zero Tracking Guarantee: AutoProfile never records your movement history or uploads your location coordinates to any remote server.

B. Do Not Disturb (DND) / Notification Policy Access

  • Permission Requested: ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY.
  • Purpose: To allow the app to silence ringer audio streams, engage Priority Only mode, or trigger Total Silence according to your active rules.
  • Protection: AutoProfile does not read, access, or log the text or sender of incoming notifications.

C. Wi-Fi & Nearby Network State

  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES.
  • Purpose: To trigger profiles when connecting to or disconnecting from specific Wi-Fi SSIDs (e.g. switching to Silent when connected to your office Wi-Fi).

D. Bluetooth Connections

  • Permissions Requested: BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT.
  • Purpose: To trigger driving or media profiles when your device connects to designated Bluetooth accessories (e.g., your car audio system or wireless headphones).

E. Calendar Events

  • Permission Requested: READ_CALENDAR.
  • Purpose: To optionally silence your device during scheduled meetings matching keywords like "Meeting", "Lecture", or "Doctor".
  • Protection: Only event start and end timestamps are queried locally. No attendee lists, notes, or event details are ever collected or transmitted.

F. Background Scheduling & Boot Restoration

  • Permissions Requested: RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK.
  • Purpose: Re-registers your scheduled profile timers after phone reboots and executes time-based profile transitions reliably without intrusive exact alarm requirements.

3. Data Storage & User Control

  • Private Sandboxed Storage: All profile configs, zone boundaries, and history logs are stored strictly inside the app's sandboxed local database (autoprofile.db).
  • Complete User Deletion: You can purge all logs, zones, and custom rules at any time via Settings → Data & Backup → Clear All Local History & State. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local records from your phone.
  • Offline Local Backup: You can export and import your rules via offline JSON files on your device storage without any cloud dependency.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

AutoProfile integrates the following privacy-compliant developer libraries and services:

  • Google Play Services (Location & Billing): Used for hardware-assisted geofencing and optional Google Play in-app purchases. Operates under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements to users on the free tier. Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) and diagnostic telemetry to serve ads in compliance with the Google Ads Privacy Policy. Pro users receive an ad-free experience.
  • OpenStreetMap (Tile & Nominatim Geocoding): Used for interactive map rendering and reverse-geocoding place names directly from your device over encrypted HTTPS under the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoProfile is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
