Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Echo Runner ("Application") for mobile devices, created by the Echo Runner Team.

1. What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

User Provided Information: The Application does not require any user registration. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information that you provide.

Automatically Collected Information: The Application does not collect information about your mobile device identity, location, or usage patterns automatically, except for anonymous statistics collected by third-party services detailed below.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the development of Echo Runner, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the Application. Google AdMob may collect and use anonymous data, such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID)
  • Approximate location data (IP address)
  • Ad interaction logs and performance parameters

For more information, please read the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Local Storage

All game save files, including your coin balances, high scores, and purchased skins, are saved locally on your device via sandbox web storage. No local save progress is uploaded to external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should contact us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
