Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

🛡️ Zero-Log Guarantee

OpenProxy Shield is committed to protecting your online privacy. We do not inspect, record, log, monitor, or store your browsing traffic, destination websites, DNS lookups, or user activity. All network operations are conducted locally on your device.

1. VPN Service and Network Routing

Our application uses Android's native VpnService API (android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE) to create a local, encrypted network loopback tunnel (127.0.0.1) on your device. This is the core functionality required to:

  • Route outbound HTTP and HTTPS socket traffic through user-selected global proxy nodes.
  • Encapsulate connection payloads and obscure your origin IP address.
  • Route DNS queries to public secure DNS resolvers (e.g., Google 8.8.8.8) to prevent ISP DNS poisoning.

We do not operate central data interception servers. We do not log which servers you connect to, what URLs you request, or what files you download.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

OpenProxy Shield is supported by advertising to offer free, unlimited proxy access. We integrate the official Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK.

AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device telemetry to serve advertisements:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • General location (IP-based city/country level)
  • App interaction metrics and diagnostic crash data

Ad traffic is explicitly bypassed from the VPN tunnel so advertising providers never monitor your encrypted proxy sessions.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

3. Local Device Data Storage

We do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal profiles. App settings such as:

  • Selected proxy node preferences and custom port settings
  • Session timer extension counters
  • Protocol filters

are saved strictly on your local device hardware using encrypted Android SharedPreferences and are never transmitted to external cloud databases.

4. Children's Privacy

OpenProxy Shield is intended for general audiences aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information from a minor has been provided, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because OpenProxy Shield does not collect, sell, or maintain personal user dossiers or server browsing logs, there is no personal profiling data available to disclose or delete. You have complete autonomy over your advertising preferences via your Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
