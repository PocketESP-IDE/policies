3. Local Processing & Vault Security

When using PDF Zenith:

  • Local Processing Engine: Merging, splitting, compressing, watermarking, password encryption, and digital signing are executed using Android native libraries and local C/C++ rendering kernels without network dependencies.
  • On-Device ML Kit OCR: Optical Character Recognition runs completely on-device using Google ML Kit local models. Extracted text remains in application memory and is never transmitted off-device.
  • Secure Vault Protection: Sensitive documents stored in the Secure Vault are isolated within the app's private sandbox storage and guarded by your device-local Vault PIN.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

PDF Zenith adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege, requesting only permissions essential to provide user-requested features:

  • android.permission.CAMERA: Used exclusively when you choose to use the Document Scanner to photograph pages. The camera hardware feature is optional (android.hardware.camera.autofocus required="false").
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used solely to request and render advertisements from Google AdMob and download user-selected open-source typography fonts. Core PDF tools operate with full functionality even when offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Triggers tactile haptic feedback when turning pages in the 3D flipbook, organizing pages, or drawing digital signatures.
  • Modern Storage Access Framework (SAF): PDF Zenith does NOT request broad legacy storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Instead, it uses Android's modern SAF and Photo Picker, granting the app access only to the explicit files you select.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the continuous development and free access of PDF Zenith, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob):

  • Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), general IP address, and ad interaction telemetry.
  • AdMob operates in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and Google Privacy Policy.
  • User Choice & Control: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.

6. User-Initiated Sharing

When you use the "Share" or "Export" feature, PDF Zenith utilizes the standard Android Share Sheet (Intent.ACTION_SEND). The selected PDF file is passed strictly to the recipient app chosen directly by you (such as your email client, cloud storage provider, or messaging app).

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because PDF Zenith does not maintain any backend database or user accounts:

  • All documents, reading progress, favorites, and settings exist solely on your physical device.
  • You can delete any document individually inside the app at any time.
  • To permanently erase all local application data, app cache, and vault files, uninstall the application or choose "Clear Storage / Clear Data" in Android Settings > Apps > PDF Zenith.

8. Children's Privacy

PDF Zenith does not address or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

9. Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & Global Rights)

Under GDPR and CCPA, users have rights regarding access, deletion, and non-discrimination. Because PDF Zenith retains no personal records or identity profiles, users maintain total custody and control over their data at all times directly on their devices.
