Our Core Privacy Commitment

Screenshot Brain is an offline-first, on-device application. Your screenshots, extracted text, tags, categorized data, and personal information never leave your phone. We do not operate remote databases that store your gallery images or OCR scan contents.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Screenshot Brain accesses and processes the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Images & Screenshots: When you grant storage permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), the app scans your device's screenshot directory to categorize and organize them.
  • Extracted Text (On-Device OCR): Text inside your screenshots is parsed directly on your phone's processor using Google ML Kit's on-device models. No image pixels or OCR transcripts are transmitted over the internet to external servers.
  • Encrypted Vault: Screenshots placed in the private vault are encrypted locally with hardware-backed biometric security.

2. Permissions Requested & Why

  • Storage / Photos Access: Required to discover and display screenshot photos from your gallery.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to notify you of calendar deadlines, extracted bill due dates, or OTP expiries found in your screenshots.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to schedule precise reminders for upcoming bills or events detected in screenshots.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint: Required solely to authenticate you before unlocking the local Private Vault.
  • Network & Internet: Used exclusively for Google Play In-App Billing (Pro subscriptions/purchases) and serving ads via Google AdMob (for free-tier users).

3. Third-Party Services

The app integrates standard Google Play developer libraries that may process limited technical metadata:

  • Google Play Billing: Handles in-app purchases securely through your Google Play account. We do not store or see your credit card or financial credentials.
  • Google AdMob: Serves standard advertisements on the free tier. AdMob may collect device identifiers and technical network diagnostics per Google's advertising policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all indexed screenshot data is stored in a local SQLite/Room database on your device, you have complete and permanent control over your data:

  • You can clear all indexed OCR data anytime inside Settings → Clear All Indexed Data.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local databases, cached indices, and app-specific preferences permanently from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Screenshot Brain does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
